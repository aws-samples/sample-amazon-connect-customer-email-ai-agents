Reservation Modification Policy

Guests may modify check-in or check-out dates up to 24 hours before
the scheduled arrival at no additional charge.

Date changes requested within 24 hours of arrival are subject to
availability and incur a $50 modification fee.

To modify a reservation, agents should:
1. Verify the booking reference number.
2. Check availability for the new dates.
3. Confirm the updated reservation details with the guest by email.
